# Art brief - Arcane Orders

Colleges, covens and familiars. Time bends around a wizard who has outlived their own school.

Doctrine: **Spellshift** (Age +18%, Power -3%)

Frame colour: `#4C6FD1`  |  Cards: 11

Shared style: hand-painted fantasy plate, 3:4 portrait, single subject, dark vignette,
rim light matching the frame colour, no text in the art (the UI draws the frame).
